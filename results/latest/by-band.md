the band, over every _n_ timed; the floor is the same for the fastest of all the algorithms
at each point. ? marks an algorithm not timed at some points in the band, with the smallest
_k_ it was not timed at, because it went over the time limit at a smaller _k_. Generated by
[`python/summarise`](../../python/summarise)
`--every-algorithm`; see [choosing algorithms](../../choosing.md).
