# Pairwise comparisons: ratio of times

The entry in a row and column is the row algorithm's worst time as a multiple of the column
algorithm's, and the _n_ and _k_ where it happens. An entry below 1× means the row algorithm was
faster at every point. A ratio overstates differences where both times are small; the
[difference in time/_k_](pairwise-difference.md) shows those better.

Each table compares every pair of algorithms in one of the [benchmark](../../results.md)
cases, over the points with _k_ ≥ 10 that both timed. I stop timing an algorithm at larger values
of _k_ for the same _n_ once a single call takes more than 20 seconds, and at that _k_ and all
larger ones for every later _n_ once that has happened at the same _k_ for two values of _n_. ?
means the row algorithm was not timed at some points where the column algorithm was, so its worst case
is unknown; the smallest such _k_ is given.

This page is generated by [`python/pairwise`](../../python/pairwise).
